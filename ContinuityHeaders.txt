Rules:  
- Record only information established in the manuscript.
- Record observations, not explanations.
- Separate reader knowledge from character knowledge.
- Record chapter-specific reveals.
- Track unresolved questions.
- Track Valley rules and mythology.
  - Shapeshifting and clothing rules currently in CharacterMap.md until Sahbobuk.md is added.
- Track timeline anchors
  - Including ages of characters if given or can be inferred
  - Travel times between locations
  - Seasonal changes and/or times of day
  - Approximate time of flashbacks or recalled events (like 14 years before current timeline, or a month ago, etc.)
- Track physical world changes.
- Do not record author-only information.
- Use LocationMap.md for referencing location names.
- Flashback / Memory / Dream Sequence Rule
  - Because The Valley Recalls is heavily built around memories, flashbacks, and dreamlike recollections, continuity entries must distinguish between information learned in the present timeline and information revealed through remembered events.
  - Timeline Anchors
    - Events occurring in flashbacks, memories, dreams, or recollections are recorded in past tense.
    - Events occurring in the chapter's present timeline are recorded in present tense.
    - The tense difference serves as an indicator of timeline location and should be preserved consistently.
  - Reader Knowledge Gained
    - Information revealed through a memory sequence is still listed because the reader has learned it.
    - Facts from memories should be written in a way that reflects they occurred in the past.
      - Example:
        Lea was married.
        Lea had a school-age daughter.
    - Present-day discoveries remain in present tense.
  - Character Knowledge Changes
    - Information a character already knew because they experienced it in the past belongs in Before.
    - Information remembered, rediscovered, reinterpreted, or newly learned during the chapter belongs in After only if the character's understanding changes.
    - A flashback itself is not automatically a knowledge change.
  - Relationships
    - Relationship states revealed through memories are recorded as historical relationship information.
    - Present-day relationship changes are documented separately.
  - Physical Changes
    - Historical injuries, transformations, pregnancies, aging states, deaths, or environmental conditions shown in memories can be recorded when first revealed to the reader.
    - The entry should clearly indicate the event occurred in the past.
  - Open Questions and Resolutions
    - A flashback can answer an existing question.
    - The answer should be documented regardless of timeline.
