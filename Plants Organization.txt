Categories for flowers, shrubs, bushes, and trees that have scent

Scent–Aesthetic:  
Only plants with no edible use and no medicinal use.
Example: Bluebells, Shooting Stars, Scarlet Gilia, Blanketflower, Asters, Goldenrod, Blue Flax (for now).

Medicinal:  
Any plant with medicinal use, whether or not it’s pretty or smells.
Example: Dandelion, Columbine, Red Clover, Plantain, Mallow, Yarrow, Sweet Clover, Fireweed, Wild Bergamot, etc.

Edible:  
Any plant with edible blossoms (or edible above‑ground parts in this flowers file).
Example: Dandelion, Wild Violets, White Clover, Red Clover, Plantain, Mallow, Wild Strawberry, Honeysuckle nectar, Evening Primrose blossoms, Sage, Wild Mustard, Sweet Clover blossoms, Fireweed blossoms, Wild Onion, Wild Garlic, Chicory, Wild Bergamot, etc.

No plant appears in Scent–Aesthetic if it’s medicinal or edible.
Medicinal and Edible are the only categories with overlap.

Rule 1 — Scent–Aesthetic is the base category.
Only plants that are there for scent and beauty go here.
If it is pretty to look at and smells and has no other use it goes here. 
Otherwise all others follow the rules below.

Rule 2: If a plant is medicinal → it is automatically scent–aesthetic.
Because all medicinal flowering plants have blossoms and are assumed to have a scent.
All plants under medicinal will be detailed in the Sahbobuk.md.

Rule 3: If a plant is edible → it is automatically scent–aesthetic.
Because edible blossoms are still blossoms and are assumed to have a scent.
All edible plants will also be cross-referenced in the appropriate Story Bible.md (Trees, Plants, Fish, etc.)

Rule 4: Edible ≠ medicinal
Some edible blossoms have no medicinal use.

Rule 5: Medicinal ≠ edible
Some medicinal blossoms are not edible.

Rule 5: We duplicate entries across categories when appropriate.
This keeps the file readable and consistent.

Rule 6 — Date sorting is strict:
Date ranges are when blossoms first appear, or leaves and/or roots/bulbs are edible/medicninal if no blossoms, through the last harvest date.
Date rangers in the Flowers-Plant.md will differ from crops espicially where blossoms turn to fruits, nuts, or seeds.
Earliest to latest
Sort by start month first, then end month.
(MAR–SEP → APR–JUN → MAY–SEP → JUN–SEP → JUL–SEP → AUG–SEP)
(MAR → MAR–APR → MAR–JUN → MAR–SEP → APR–SEP → MAY–JUN → MAY–SEP → JUN–SEP → AUG–SEP)
Alphabetical for same dates.

Rule 7 — Formatting the bullets under the headers:
For all entries the formatting is this format:
**Plant Name** (Date Range(s)); where ** are dropped and just designates where to put the plant name and does NOT mean bold it.
Specific flowchart formats are listed between "__".
Example: Wild mint (JUL–SEP)
Keep naming consistent across Story Bible md files.
