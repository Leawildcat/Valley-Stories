Most Popular Shade Trees in Chama and Ruidoso, New Mexico:

Blue Spruce (Picea pungens) – Evergreen, provides year-round shade and a striking blue foliage.
Western Chokecherry (Prunus virginiana) – Deciduous, small to medium size, with ornamental spring flowers and edible fruit.
Douglas Fir (Pseudotsuga menziesii) – Large, long-lived evergreen with dense shade.
Quaking Aspen (Populus tremuloides) – Fast-growing deciduous with a wide, open canopy.
White Fir (Abies concolor) – Evergreen with a graceful, open form.
Ponderosa Pine (Pinus ponderosa) – Large evergreen with a broad, spreading crown.
Fremont Cottonwood (Populus fremontii) – Fast-growing deciduous, good for riparian areas.
Rocky Mountain Juniper (Juniperus scopulorum) – Evergreen, drought-tolerant, and shade-providing.
Box Elder (Acer negundo) – Fast-growing deciduous with large leaves.
Limber Pine (Pinus flexilis) – Evergreen, adapted to high elevations.
Bigtooth Maple (Acer grandidentatum) – Deciduous with large, maple-like leaves.
Blue Elderberry (Sambucus caerulea) – Deciduous, ornamental and edible.
Velvet Ash (Fraxinus velutina) – Deciduous, tolerant of poor soils.
Gambel Oak (Quercus gambelii) – Deciduous, drought-tolerant, and fire-resistant.
New Mexico Locust (Robinia neomexicana) – Fast-growing deciduous with ornamental flowers.
Wright’s Silktassel (Garrya wrightii) – Deciduous, drought-tolerant, and ornamental.
Arizona Walnut (Juglans major) – Large deciduous with ornamental bark and edible nuts.
Western Redbud (Cercis occidentalis) – Small deciduous with pink spring flowers.
Catclaw Acacia (Acacia greggii) – Deciduous, drought-tolerant, and ornamental.
Mimosa (Albizia julibrissin var. rosea) – Fast-growing deciduous with ornamental flowers.
New Mexico Olive (Forestiera neomexicana) – Deciduous, drought-tolerant.
Littleleaf Ash (Fraxinus gregii) – Deciduous, tolerant of poor soils.
Mesquite (Prosopis glandulosa) – Deciduous, drought-tolerant, and shade-providing.
Chokecherry (Prunus virginiana var. melanocarpa) – Deciduous, ornamental and edible.
Hoptree (Ptelea trifoliata) – Deciduous, ornamental.
Buckthorn (Rhamnus cathartica) – Deciduous, ornamental.
Mescal Bean (Sophora secundiflora) – Deciduous, ornamental.
Desert Willow (Chilopsis linearis) – Deciduous, ornamental flowers, good shade.
Chaste Tree (Vitex agnus-castus) – Deciduous, ornamental.
