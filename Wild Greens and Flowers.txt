## 🌿 **Wild Sage = Dual‑Category Plant (Scent‑Hybrid)**  
Here’s the clean rule you just articulated:

### **1. Sage has a scent even when not blooming.**  
That’s leaf‑based, resin‑based, herbal.  
This belongs in **Non‑Flowering Scent Plants**.

### **2. But when sage *is* blooming, the blossoms amplify the scent so strongly that the bloom period becomes a distinct scent profile.**  
That’s blossom‑based, seasonal, floral‑enhanced.  
This belongs in **Flowering Scent Plants**.

So Wild Sage is a **hybrid** — a plant that legitimately belongs in *both* categories because:

- It has a **baseline scent** (leaves/stems)  
- It has a **bloom‑enhanced scent** (flowers)  
- The bloom scent is strong enough to be considered its own profile  
- The bloom scent is *not* just “baby leaves” like mint  
- The bloom scent changes how the plant interacts with the environment (you smell sage bloom before you see it)

This is canon‑consistent and matches how you’ve described it in the Valley.

### ✔ This means Wild Sage gets two entries:
- **Non‑Flowering Scent Plants:** “Wild Sage — leaf/resin scent (year‑round presence)”  
- **Flowering Scent Plants:** “Wild Sage Blossoms — amplified sage scent (MAY–SEP)”  

This is not duplication — it’s classification by scent source.

Mint does **not** get this dual treatment because its flowers don’t change the scent profile.  
Sage does.

So we now have our first **special rule** for Flowers‑Plants.md:

## 🌼 **Special Rule: Scent‑Hybrid Plants**  
Some plants produce a scent from leaves/stems *and* a distinct scent from blossoms.  
These plants appear in **both** categories.

Criteria:
- Leaf/stem scent is strong enough to classify independently  
- Blossom scent is strong enough to classify independently  
- Blossom scent is *not* merely an extension of the leaf scent  
- Bloom period creates a noticeable environmental scent shift  
- Bloom scent is used in canon (ritual, aesthetic, or food)

Examples (conceptual only):
- Wild Sage  
- Possibly Wild Bergamot (depending on how you classify leaf vs blossom scent)  
- Possibly Bee Plant (if you decide its bloom scent is distinct)
